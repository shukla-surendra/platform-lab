# Flashcards

Spaced-repetition review built from the word banks. Pick your decks, try to recall each
meaning, then rate how well you knew it. Cards you find hard come back sooner, and cards you
know well come back less often. Your progress is saved in this browser only.

??? tip "Keyboard shortcuts"
    | Key | Action |
    |---|---|
    | <kbd>Space</kbd> / <kbd>Enter</kbd> | Show the meaning |
    | <kbd>1</kbd> <kbd>2</kbd> <kbd>3</kbd> <kbd>4</kbd> | Again · Hard · Good · Easy |
    | <kbd>V</kbd> | Hear the pronunciation |
    | <kbd>→</kbd> | Skip this card |
