# Book Summaries

Deliberate, detailed summaries of business/strategy/engineering-leadership books — not back-cover blurbs. Each one follows this repo's own conventions (`CLAUDE.md`): index + anchors, a glossary table for non-trivial vocabulary, and cross-links into `Project_Management/` and `Communication-Mastery/` wherever the book's argument overlaps material already built out there, rather than duplicating it.

## Books

| Title | Author(s) | Core topic | Summary |
|---|---|---|---|
| *What I Didn't Learn in Business School: How Strategy Works in the Real World* | Jay B. Barney, Trish Gorman Clifford | Why strategy frameworks (VRIO, Five Forces) are necessary but not sufficient — the political/stakeholder layer that actually determines whether a correct recommendation gets implemented | [`what_i_didnt_learn_in_business_school.md`](what_i_didnt_learn_in_business_school.md) |
| *Silos, Politics and Turf Wars: A Leadership Fable About Destroying the Barriers That Turn Colleagues Into Competitors* | Patrick Lencioni | Why cross-team conflict is usually a missing-shared-goal problem, not a people problem — the Thematic Goal / Defining Objectives / Standard Operating Objectives framework for removing it | [`silos_politics_and_turf_wars.md`](silos_politics_and_turf_wars.md) |
| *A Sense of Urgency* | John P. Kotter | Why change efforts fail before they start — true urgency (determination) vs. false urgency (anxiety) vs. complacency, and the four tactics for building the real thing | [`a_sense_of_urgency.md`](a_sense_of_urgency.md) |
| *The No Asshole Rule* | Dr. Robert I. Sutton | The power-differential test for demeaning behavior, its measurable organizational cost, the 10-step framework for building a no-jerk culture, and how to survive one you can't yet fix | [`the_no_asshole_rule.md`](the_no_asshole_rule.md) |
| *Ultralearning: Master Hard Skills, Outsmart the Competition, and Accelerate Your Career* | Scott Young | Nine principles for self-directed skill acquisition (meta-learning, focus, directness, drill, retrieval, feedback, retention, intuition, experimentation) — applied to articulation/DSA/system-design prep, plus a general-purpose checklist for any skill | [`ultralearning.md`](ultralearning.md) |
