(which is original, authored content with an "Articulate It" section per
doc — see `fundamentals/CLAUDE.md`), these files are aggregated Q&A for
quick reference/review, not first-principles tutorials. No "Articulate It"

## Known gaps (called out honestly rather than papered over)

- **`mlops.md`**: some categories have full Q&A, two are unanswered question
  lists with no answer key, and a few categories had no content available.
- **`ai_engineering.md`**: most entries link out to an external resource for
  the answer rather than including one inline. Entries without any answer
  resource are explicitly marked as such rather than invented.
- **`azure.md`** and **`llmops.md`**: only a subset of the advertised
  question count actually had complete answers available; the rest were not
  accessible. Only the available ones are included, in full.
- **`gcp.md`** and **`aws.md`**: one or more of the original references for
  each topic could not be retrieved at all; the remaining reference(s) are
  included in full.
