Workers — heartbeat every 1s, lease 5s

How heartbeats, acks and leases work
  • Claim = receipt ack. A worker takes the next job with FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED, bumps attempts and gets a 5s lease.
  • Heartbeat. Every second the worker marks itself alive and, if busy, pushes the lease 5s forward and reports progress. It also reads commands: pause for itself, cancel for its job.
  • Ack / nack. On finish it writes succeeded (ack) or a failure (nack): back to queued with a 2s, 4s, 8s… backoff, or dead after the last attempt. A cancel is acknowledged the same way.
  • Lease expiry. No heartbeat for 5s (crash, stall) and any worker may reclaim the job. The old worker's late writes check attempts and match nothing (fencing).
