Point OpenTelemetry exporters at it:

  OTEL_EXPORTER_OTLP_ENDPOINT=http://{{ include "rust-sqlite-api-stack.fullname" . }}.{{ .Release.Namespace }}.svc:{{ .Values.service.port }}
  OTEL_EXPORTER_OTLP_PROTOCOL=http/json

  Note: http/json, not the gRPC most SDKs default to. This collector speaks
  OTLP over HTTP with a JSON body only.

Look at it locally:

  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} port-forward svc/{{ include "rust-sqlite-api-stack.fullname" . }} {{ .Values.service.port }}:{{ .Values.service.port }}
  curl localhost:{{ .Values.service.port }}/api/summary
  curl localhost:{{ .Values.service.port }}/metrics

volume — extra replicas do not share it, they fragment your telemetry across
disconnected databases. The chart refuses to render above 1.
